# Executive Summary – Logistics KPI Analysis

**Date:** Prepared as Portfolio Project  
**Focus:** Operational Performance in Logistics & Asset Deployment

---

## 1. Key Findings at a Glance

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Logistics Delay Rate | **56.6%** | High – requires immediate process attention |
| Average Asset Utilization | **79.6%** | Moderate – potential for 10–15% improvement |
| Average Waiting Time | **35 minutes** | Significant idle time opportunity |
| Most Common Delay Reasons | Traffic, Weather | Controllable through better planning & routing |

---

## 2. Root Cause Highlights

- **Traffic-related delays** and **Heavy / Detour traffic status** strongly correlate with delayed shipments.
- Assets with lower utilization still experience high waiting times → process inefficiencies beyond pure capacity.
- Inventory levels and Demand Forecast show opportunities for better alignment to reduce stockouts or overstock situations that can cascade into logistics delays.

---

## 3. Recommended Next Actions (Process Improvement)

1. **Daily KPI Stand-up Focus**
   - Track Delay Rate, Waiting Time, and Top 3 Delay Reasons every morning.
   - Escalate when Delay Rate exceeds a defined threshold (e.g., >45%).

2. **Targeted Root Cause Deep-Dives**
   - Prioritize Traffic & Weather-related delays for contingency planning.
   - Review route optimization and carrier performance.

3. **Asset Utilization Improvement**
   - Analyze low-utilization assets for reallocation or process redesign.
   - Link utilization to waiting time reduction initiatives.

4. **Ad-hoc Reporting Capability**
   - Maintain a clean data pipeline so stakeholders (RAD Ops, Transport, BU) can receive rapid insights.

- Systematic KPI definition and tracking  
- Root cause analysis using operational data  
- Clear visualization and communication of findings  
- Action-oriented recommendations suitable for leadership review
